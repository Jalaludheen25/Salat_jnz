# Product Overview

**Janaza Guide** (Salat Al Janaza) is a Flutter mobile application that provides comprehensive guidance for Islamic funeral rites and procedures. The app serves as a digital guide for Muslims to understand and follow proper Islamic practices related to death, funeral prayers, and burial procedures.

## Core Features

- **Mayyith Samskaranam**: Guidance on preparing the deceased
- **Bathing the Corpse**: Step-by-step instructions for ritual washing
- **Kafan**: Instructions for shrouding the deceased
- **Salat Al-Janaza**: Funeral prayer guidance with audio support
- **Post-Funeral Care**: Information about post-burial practices
- **Gender-Specific Instructions**: Separate guidance for male and female procedures

## Target Audience

The app is designed for Malayalam-speaking Muslim communities, providing religious guidance in their native language with Arabic text and transliterations where appropriate.

## Key Characteristics

- Multilingual support (Malayalam, Arabic, English)
- Audio integration for prayers and recitations
- Step-by-step visual guides with illustrations
- Offline functionality for essential content
- Respectful, educational approach to sensitive religious topics
